# CommerceScout AI project brief

CommerceScout AI reduces repetitive early-stage product research for e-commerce operators. The MVP gathers available catalog evidence, normalizes it, makes evidence gaps visible, applies transparent ranking criteria, and retains a reviewable report.

Its intended users are Shopify operators, product marketers, and engineers evaluating AI-assisted workflows. The product does not promise profitable products, demand, sales, or product-market fit.

MVP success means a user can submit a bounded research request; a worker can persist and process it; product evidence and scores can be inspected; errors and cancellations are honest; and a CSV can be exported. Fixture mode must demonstrate the workflow without credentials. Live mode adds approved browser collection and Claude-directed use of restricted MCP tools when credentials are available.
